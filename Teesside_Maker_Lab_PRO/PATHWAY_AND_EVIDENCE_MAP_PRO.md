# BUILD / GROW / LAUNCH and evidence map

## Constant artefact, changing reasoning demand

The suite does not give BUILD a childish artefact and LAUNCH a prestigious one. All pathways can make the same high-impact object. The adaptation is in decision count, language, uncertainty, comparison and evaluation.

| Dimension | BUILD | GROW | LAUNCH |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interface demand | One decision at a time; large targets; concrete labels | Two-variable comparison; visible trade-offs | Multi-variable optimisation and uncertainty |
| Prediction | Direction or category with a brief reason | Direction plus approximate value | Quantitative estimate, confidence and assumptions |
| Testing | Immediate visual cause and effect | Compare planned and observed result | Evaluate validity, sensitivity and model limits |
| Revision | Change one named feature | Compare before/after and explain effect | Justify a revision against competing criteria |
| Humanities | Notice a person, place, object or constraint | Explain a relationship or viewpoint | Evaluate claims, source status and counter-perspective |
| Evidence | Photo/screenshot plus pupil sentence | Prediction, test and visible revision | Full decision replay, measures, source boundary and evaluation |

## Evidence categories supported

- **Process:** initial choice, prediction and design timeline.
- **Technical:** measurements, tolerances, route, transfer, contour or economic model result.
- **Creative:** composition, material vocabulary, visual intention and interpretation.
- **Humanities:** source status, claim, perspective, context and limitation.
- **Revision:** Design A versus current design, with pupil explanation.
- **Physical bridge:** fabrication output, photographed artefact or physical-proof comparison.
- **Reflection:** what changed, why, and what the model could not establish.

The suite organises evidence. The centre remains responsible for mapping evidence to current programme or qualification requirements.
