Professional systems shared across the suite

These are working systems, not decorative dashboards.

Prediction-Locked Tests

Specialist tests stay concealed until the learner commits an expected direction, confidence and reason. The result calibrates judgement without grading confidence itself.

Design Replay

Undo/redo history, baseline checkpoints, frozen Design A and metric comparisons preserve what changed and why rather than showing only a polished endpoint.

Physical Reconciliation

Shadow photographs, physical print captures and fabrication calibration checks let the real artefact challenge the model while keeping images local.

Cross-App Translation

Token → economy, hydro-board → navigation and relief → collagraph relays transform representations while explicitly recording that a model transfer is not historical proof.

Workshop safety boundary

01No operational hot-metal temperatures, pouring sequence, quench process or alloy recipe is encoded.
02No reactive oxidation mixture, powder specification or activation timing is provided.
03CAM, contour and mechanism exports are craft planning templates, not structural or navigational certification.

Local-first privacy

ANo account, API, external library, analytics service or runtime network request is required.
BUploaded photos stay inside the browser/project export unless the user deliberately saves a dossier.
CPortable .makerlab, .makerstudio, .makerclass and .makerhub files support managed-device handoff.
