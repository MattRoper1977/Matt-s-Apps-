# Professional teacher workshop matrix

| Studio | Professional enquiry event | Physical bridge | High-value evidence | BUILD calibration | GROW calibration | LAUNCH calibration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 Shadow Rig | Predict shadow fit, run a tolerance storm, revise the highest-impact node | Three-plane wire/card armature and fixed teacher-approved lamp | Prediction, robustness result, photographed physical-shadow comparison, revised assembly order | Move one node and name the visible effect | Compare two node changes and explain which is more robust | Evaluate sensitivity, tolerance and model limitations |
| 02 Token Foundry | Diagnose topology, flow, release and front/back registration | Cold-craft layered card or embossing matrix; any hot process remains separately controlled | Two-sided design, diagnostic result, revised matrix, historical-source boundary | Choose one symbol and one relief band | Compare two layout changes | Justify topology, registration and evidence limitations |
| 03 Smuggling Rig | Spend limited soundings, lock a route, autopsy failure/success | Aged chart and hidden magnetic slider channel | Probe decisions, route prediction, live run, autopsy, revised channel | Select safe/unknown cells and follow a short route | Compare tide and draught trade-offs | Evaluate uncertainty, route efficiency and historical inference |
| 04 Relief Lab | Test composition under raking light, distance and release conditions | Shallow card/clay/plaster relief using setting-approved materials | Light test, release X-ray, distance test, exhibition label | Place and move large forms | Compare texture/depth choices | Evaluate readability, release risk and urban narrative |
| 05 Steelplate CAM | Solve text/rivet constraints and run tolerance analysis | Aluminium tooling foil or heavy card emboss/punch pattern | Constraint result, punch route, tolerance pass rate, tiled calibration pack | Choose text and follow marked centres | Compare diameter/margin/text-fit effects | Explain heuristic limits, tolerance and fabrication strategy |
| 06 Hydro-Board | Choose sparse soundings, infer contours, reveal error | Layered contour card and conceptual cam-crank tide board | Survey plan, error map, nested layers, cam profile | Take guided soundings and identify deep/shallow | Compare sample locations and interval choices | Evaluate interpolation error, sample efficiency and model validity |
| 07 Collagraph | Pull an edition, observe matrix wear, compare a physical proof | Textured collagraph matrix and setting-approved print process | Predicted proof, edition graph, physical-photo region comparison, sensory station plan | Select two textures and make one clear revision | Compare pressure/ink/material transfer | Evaluate edition consistency, wear and model-to-print discrepancy |
| 08 Economy Lab | Run baseline, lock shock prediction, test reforms and perspectives | Token/object handling, archive source work and structured argument | Ledger, calibration, policy comparison, perspective tribunal, source board | Identify what can/cannot be bought | Compare two household conditions | Evaluate counterfactual assumptions and triangulate source claims |

## Suggested session grammar

- **5 minutes — Understand:** artefact hook and source status.
- **5 minutes — Predict:** learner commits to expected result.
- **15 minutes — Make:** digital design and/or physical construction.
- **7 minutes — Test:** reveal the app-specific diagnostic or physical comparison.
- **5 minutes — Revise:** freeze Design A, alter one defensible feature and compare.
- **3 minutes — Explain:** capture the learner’s change, reason and evidence.

Timings are planning prompts, not a mandatory lesson script.
