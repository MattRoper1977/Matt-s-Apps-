Not a safeguarding record

Where a child may be unsafe, staff must follow the setting’s approved safeguarding process immediately. The flagship can mark a transfer and redact the local voice; it must never delay or replace that process.

Not secure cloud case management

Records remain in the local browser profile. Use aliases, minimise information, define retention, protect exports and transfer anything requiring formal retention into an approved organisational system.
