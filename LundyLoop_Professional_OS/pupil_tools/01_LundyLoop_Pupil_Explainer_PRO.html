The Lundy Loop: Your Voice, Real Change

Pupil explainer · UNCRC Article 12 participation model

Safe Space → Your Words → The Right Ears → Real Change

You can share a view in a way that works for you. Adults should make sure it reaches someone who can consider it, then tell you what happened. Getting a clear reason or a Plan B can be part of a real loop too.

No pressure to speakDifferent ways to communicateA named listenerA response comes back

🛋️ 1. Safe Space

A safe opportunity comes first. You can think before answering, choose how to respond, or say that you do not want to answer right now.

✓ You can ask for time.You do not have to answer instantly.
✓ You can use another method.Speaking is not the only way to have a voice.
✓ You can ask who will hear it.The audience should be clear.
✓ You can ask what happened next.Feedback is part of influence.

My Lundy Voice & Rights Card

Article 12: children and young people have a right to express views on matters affecting them and for those views to be given due weight according to age and maturity.

1 · SAFE SPACE
I need a real chance to think and form a view.
2 · YOUR WORDS
I can communicate my view in a way that works for me.
3 · THE RIGHT EARS
My view should reach someone who can consider it.
4 · REAL CHANGE
I should find out what happened because of what I said.
Things I can ask: “Can I have more time?” · “Can I show you another way?” · “Who will hear this?” · “What happened next?”
