Safeguarding & data boundary

What this tool is not

Not a safeguarding record: do not delay or replace your setting’s safeguarding process. Detailed disclosures should move to the approved system and designated safeguarding lead.
Not secure cloud case management: IndexedDB is local browser storage. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to access or delete it.
Data minimisation: use aliases, collect only what is needed, maintain accuracy, define retention and protect exports.
