How Data Manager Studio works

  1. Create a cohort and learners.
  2. Create a programme and unit, pasting exact current requirements.
  3. Capture or import draft evidence and link it to lesson context.
  4. Use coverage to find gaps; a count is not an achievement decision.
  5. Review records, then use your centre’s official process.
  6. Back up regularly to an approved secure location.
Important boundaryThis is not AQA, ASDAN, Trinity or another awarding body’s portal and cannot certify, moderate or submit claims.
