# LundyLoop Pupil Suite — Staff Delivery Guide

## Five-minute introduction

1. **Safe Space** — “We are not putting you on the spot. You can have time to think and you can use a way of communicating that works for you.”
2. **Your Words** — “We need your actual view. If an adult adds an interpretation, we should label it as the adult’s interpretation rather than pretending those were your exact words.”
3. **The Right Ears** — “A view needs to reach someone who can genuinely consider it or respond. You can ask who that person is.”
4. **Real Change / Feedback** — “Influence does not mean adults have to say yes. It means your view is considered and you are told what happened. If something cannot happen, you should get a clear reason and, where possible, another workable route.”
5. **Close the loop** — Run one scenario in the Pupil Explainer. Stop before the outcome is returned and ask pupils whether the loop is closed. Then add the returned outcome and compare.

## Important implementation boundary

The prototypes are not secure information-management systems. The Live Class Board persists locally in the browser for convenience. Use aliases/initials in demonstrations and move any formal evidence into your organisation's approved systems.

## What counts as a closed loop in this suite

A loop is shown as closed when:

- the pupil had an opportunity to form and communicate a view;
- a relevant audience is identified;
- an action, decision or reasoned response is recorded; and
- that response is returned to the pupil.

Pupil agreement or satisfaction is recorded separately. A pupil may understand a decision and still disagree with it.
